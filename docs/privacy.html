RightConvert Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 Applies to: the RightConvert desktop app for Windows (Microsoft Store package Kodji.RightConvert, and the non-Store Setup build), published by Kodji.

Summary

RightConvert converts files locally, on your own computer, offline. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, has no telemetry or analytics, requires no account, and never uploads your files or anything else over the network.

What the app does with your files

  • Files you add (through the window, drag and drop, or the File Explorer "Convert with RightConvert" command) are read and converted on your device only.
  • Converted files are saved next to the originals or in the output folder you choose. Originals are never overwritten or deleted.
  • Short-lived temporary working folders may be created on your device during a conversion and are removed afterwards.
  • The app contains no networking code: it makes no HTTP requests, opens no network connections, does not check for updates on its own and does not contact any server. License verification, where used, is fully offline.
  • Office documents that reference external/remote resources are rejected rather than fetched.

Data stored on your device

RightConvert keeps two small things, only on your computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RightConvert:

  • settings.json – your preferences: the chosen output folder, whether to save next to the original, and the locations of FFmpeg / LibreOffice if you selected them.
  • logs\technical.log – a size-limited technical log (rotated at about 512 KB) with timestamps and short error messages such as an error type or a file extension. It does not contain file contents and is meant for troubleshooting.

When installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows automatically stores these files under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Kodji.RightConvert_h9nm9abegh7ny\LocalCache\Local\RightConvert and removes them when you uninstall the app. You can delete them at any time. Nothing in them is ever sent anywhere by RightConvert.

The app also reads (never writes) a few Windows settings locally: the light/dark app theme, and the standard LibreOffice install location in the registry / Program Files, so it can find an installed LibreOffice.

Optional external tools

RightConvert does not bundle LibreOffice or FFmpeg. If you have them installed or point the app to them, RightConvert runs them locally as separate programs on your computer to perform Office→PDF and audio/video conversions. Files are passed to them on your device; nothing is uploaded. Those tools are third-party software governed by their own licences and policies.

Data we collect

None. No personal data, usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or location are collected by RightConvert, and nothing is shared with third parties. No account or sign-in is required.

The Microsoft Store itself (downloads, purchases, ratings) is operated by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; the developer does not receive personal data from it beyond the aggregated reports Microsoft provides to all publishers.

Children

RightConvert does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Politica de confidențialitate (Română)

RightConvert convertește fișierele local, pe calculatorul tău, fără internet. Aplicația nu colectează date personale, nu are telemetrie sau analiză de utilizare, nu necesită cont și nu trimite niciun fișier sau altă informație prin rețea.

  • Fișierele sunt citite și convertite doar pe dispozitivul tău; rezultatele se salvează lângă original sau în folderul ales. Originalele nu sunt șterse sau suprascrise.
  • Setările (settings.json: folderul de ieșire, căile către FFmpeg / LibreOffice) și jurnalul tehnic (logs\technical.log, fără conținutul fișierelor) rămân pe dispozitiv, în %LOCALAPPDATA%\RightConvert (pentru versiunea din Microsoft Store, Windows le păstrează în folderul pachetului și le șterge la dezinstalare).
  • Instrumentele externe opționale (LibreOffice, FFmpeg) rulează local, pe calculatorul tău.
  • Contact: deschide un issue pe https://github.com/kodji7202-code/rightconvert/issues.
